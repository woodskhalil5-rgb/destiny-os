How it works

Up and running in three steps.

No software to learn and no setup calls. Answer a few questions and your team gets to work.

  1. Tell us about your business

    What you do, where you work, who your best customers are and what you want more of. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Your team gets set up

    Each of the six gets a job written for your business, and the team learns your services, area and rules. Connect your email and Facebook Page when you're ready.

  3. You approve, they do the work

    Every email and post waits for your OK. Approve it from your phone or computer, and watch new leads and conversations show up.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

What does the team actually do each day?

On their working hours, James finds new leads, Emily checks and sorts them, and Kole writes emails to the best ones. Grayson makes posts from your photos and videos, and Nora keeps track of who owes you and drafts payment reminders. DeMarcus runs the plan and reports to you. Anything that would go out to the world lands in your "To approve" list first.

Will anything be sent without me knowing?

No. Every email and every post waits for your approval. You can approve, edit or say no to each one, from your phone or your computer.

Do I need to be good with technology?

No. You answer a few questions when you sign up, then approve or decline what the team brings you. Connecting your email and Facebook Page takes a few minutes, and the dashboard walks you through it.

What does AI usage cost?

It depends on how much work the team does. Your dashboard shows the cost as it happens, broken down by teammate, and every business has a monthly limit. When the limit is reached, the team rests until the next month.

What can the team connect to?

Your business email (to send what you approve and catch replies), your Facebook Page (to publish approved posts), and a library for your job photos and videos.

Who can see my business?

Only you and the people you invite. Every business on Destiny OS is kept completely separate.

How do I start?

Tell us about your business and your team is set up for it right away, so you can see who does what before you pay. When you're ready, pay your first month ($650 for 30 days) in your dashboard and tap Start team. After that you're billed on the 1st of each month: the platform fee plus the AI work your team did.

How do I cancel?

Email us any time. Service ends at the end of that month, and we can export or delete your data on request. The details are in our Terms.
