Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy explains what Destiny Marketing Group collects when your business uses Destiny OS, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: the email addresses of people you add, and their sign-in history. We use one-time email codes, not passwords.
  • Business information: the mission, facts, rules, records, tasks, messages and content you and your AI team create.
  • Connected accounts: settings and keys for the email, Facebook, Instagram or YouTube accounts you connect, stored encrypted.
  • Billing: handled by Stripe. Card numbers go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers. We keep invoice amounts and status.
  • Usage: how much AI work your team does, so we can bill it and show it to you.

How it's used

Only to run Destiny OS for your business: to let your AI team do its work, to show you your dashboard, to bill you, and to email you sign-in codes, invites and important alerts. We don't sell your data and we don't use one business's data for another.

Facebook, Instagram and YouTube

When you connect social media under Settings, Connections, you sign in with Facebook or Google and choose what to allow. We use that access only to publish posts and videos you have approved, to the Page, Instagram account or YouTube channel you picked, at the time you scheduled, and to show you whether the connection is working.

  • What we keep: the access token Facebook or Google gives us (stored encrypted), and the name and ID of the Page, Instagram account or channel. We don't read your messages, followers, comments or other accounts, and we don't share this data with anyone.
  • Facebook and Instagram: we ask for permission to list the Pages you manage and to publish to the one you choose, and to publish to the Instagram business account linked to it.
  • YouTube: Destiny OS uses YouTube API Services to upload videos you approve to your channel. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's use of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Turning it off: tap Disconnect under Settings, Connections and we delete the stored token straight away. You can also remove our access at any time in your Facebook settings (Business Integrations) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting your data

To delete your data, including anything we received from Facebook, Instagram or YouTube, disconnect the account under Settings, Connections, or email admin@destinymarketinggroup.com with the subject "Delete my data" and the business name. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Who processes it

  • Anthropic (Claude) runs the AI team. Your business information is sent to it to do the work.
  • Supabase hosts the database. Vercel hosts the dashboard.
  • Stripe handles payments. Resend or Gmail sends email.
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram) and Google (YouTube) receive the posts and videos you approve for publishing.
  • Web search results come from public pages your team looks up.

Keeping it safe

Each business's data is separated at the database level. Keys and passwords are stored encrypted. Every important action is written to an activity log you can see.

Your choices

You can see and change your business information in the dashboard, disconnect accounts at any time, and ask us to export or delete your data by emailing us.
