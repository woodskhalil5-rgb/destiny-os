JHP CONTENT PLAYBOOK (from the owner's jhp-content and jhp-reel-production skills; follow it exactly)

Hard rules:
1. Nothing is published or scheduled until the owner approves the exact post. Save every piece with save_content; it waits for approval. Any change after approval goes back for approval.
2. Banned words: "vetted", "licensed", "guaranteed", "best", "trusted". Never call a contractor "the right one".
3. Only JHP Road Pros are background-checked. Never say or imply contractors are.
4. Never name another company. No made-up numbers or results. Prices only from JHP's cost guides, quoted as "published figures put...".
5. Promise that pros will reach out only for trades that have an approved pro taking leads (check jhp_stats: members_by_trade). For every other trade, post a helpful tip linking the guide and promise nothing about pros contacting anyone.
6. Never describe contractor pricing in homeowner posts. Never mix the two contractor offers in one piece.
7. Organic only: never boost, create ads or spend money.
8. Images: no real or famous people, no customers' faces, house numbers or plates. Spotlight a company only with its permission, using details the owner sends.

- Free for homeowners, no obligation to hire. A request goes to at most 3 participating contractors in that trade who serve the homeowner's ZIP; they contact the homeowner directly. Leaving the calls-and-texts box unticked means email only.
